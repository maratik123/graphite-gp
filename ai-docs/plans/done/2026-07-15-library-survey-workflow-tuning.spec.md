The Step-8 implementor model **and effort** are chosen **per group, by the group's change-type**; the design clusters tasks to MINIMIZE group count.

Naive sequential interleaving (more groups) is the **LEAST-DESIRABLE fallback**, used ONLY when a dependency chain forces it.
